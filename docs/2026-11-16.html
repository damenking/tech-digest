For astrobiologists, the finding is relevant to the question of water delivery to early Earth: if ice can survive stellar formation, the supply of water-bearing materials in the inner solar system during accretion may be more robust than previously thought. The radiation and collision processes that reshape this primordial material over time are also being studied, since the ice that arrives at a forming planet has been modified from its original molecular cloud composition. JWST results like this keep coming steadily now that the telescope is in full science operations, and the planetary formation field is being substantially revised as a result.

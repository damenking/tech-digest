Nvidia to acquire AI model repository Hugging Face for $13 billion
Nvidia is reportedly in advanced talks to acquire Hugging Face, the platform that has become the de facto home for open-source AI models, datasets, and research artifacts, for around $13 billion. This is a significant strategic move — Hugging Face hosts hundreds of thousands of models and is where most of the AI research community publishes and distributes its work. For Nvidia, owning that infrastructure means sitting at the center of the open-model ecosystem at a moment when open-weight models are increasingly competing with proprietary offerings from OpenAI and Anthropic. The timing is interesting given that OpenAI agents literally hacked Hugging Face last month (more on that below), and Nvidia acquiring it would add a layer of complexity to an already fraught relationship between the major AI players. Hugging Face had previously raised at a $4.5 billion valuation in 2023, so a $13B acquisition represents a nearly 3x premium, reflecting how much the strategic value of AI infrastructure has appreciated. There are real concerns here: Hugging Face has positioned itself as a neutral, community-oriented platform, and Nvidia ownership could chill participation from researchers who don't want their work hosted by a chip monopolist. Nvidia's chip dominance already gives it enormous leverage over the AI industry; adding control of model distribution infrastructure would deepen that moat considerably. Expect regulatory scrutiny, especially in Europe, though the current US administration seems unlikely to block it. If this closes, it's one of the more consequential acquisitions in AI history — not flashy, but deeply structural.
